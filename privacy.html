Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

Woofora ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that data when you use the Woofora mobile application.

Data We Collect

Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and (optionally) your display name. If you sign in via Google, Apple, Facebook, or Microsoft, we receive only the information those providers share (typically your name and email).

Dog Profiles: You may optionally create dog profiles containing your dog's name, breed, size, age group, weight, chew strength, and activity level. This data is used solely to personalize product recommendations.

Reviews & Wishlists: If you write product reviews or create wishlists, that content is stored in your account.

Push Notification Token: If you enable push notifications, we store an Expo push token to send you product alerts and updates. No notification content is stored on our servers.

Affiliate Click Data: When you tap a "Buy on Amazon" link, we record an anonymous click event (product ID, retailer, timestamp). No personal information is attached to click tracking.

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and personalize the Woofora service
  • To recommend products based on your dog's profile
  • To send push notifications (only if you opt in)
  • To track affiliate link clicks for Amazon Associates reporting
  • To improve the app and fix bugs

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

Woofora relies on the following third-party services to function. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Supabase — Authentication, database, and file storage. Privacy Policy
  • Expo — Push notification delivery. Privacy Policy
  • Amazon Associates — Affiliate links. When you click a product link, you are directed to Amazon. Amazon may set cookies as per their Privacy Notice.

Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your account data as long as your account is active. You may request account deletion at any time by emailing support@woofora.app. Upon deletion, all your personal data (email, dog profiles, reviews, wishlists) is permanently removed from our database.

Security

We use Supabase for authentication and data storage, which provides industry-standard encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access to user data is governed by Row-Level Security policies that ensure you can only access your own data.

Children's Privacy

Woofora is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of Woofora after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or your data, contact us at support@woofora.app.
