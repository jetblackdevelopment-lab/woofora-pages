Account Deletion

Last updated: August 2026

We're sorry to see you go. This page explains how to delete your Woofora account and exactly what data is removed.

How to request deletion

  1. Email support@woofora.app from the address you signed up with
  2. Use the subject line: Account deletion request
  3. We'll confirm and process your request within 7 business days

If you'd like to delete only some of your data (e.g. just your reviews), include Partial deletion: [data type] in the subject line and we'll walk you through the options.

What gets deleted

  • Your email address and authentication credentials
  • All dog profiles you created (name, breed, size, age, weight, chew strength, activity level)
  • All wishlists (names and the products saved in each)
  • All reviews you wrote (rating, title, body, photos)
  • Your notification preferences and registered device tokens

What we keep (anonymized)

  • Aggregated, non-identifiable click events on affiliate links — needed to pay Amazon Associates commissions correctly
  • Backup snapshots for up to 90 days (then permanently deleted)

What happens after you request deletion

  1. You receive an email confirmation within 1 business day
  2. Your account is marked for deletion and you can no longer sign in
  3. Personal data is removed from production within 7 business days
  4. Encrypted backups are purged within 90 days

Need help?

If you can't sign in to your account (forgotten password, lost access to your email), email support@woofora.app with any proof of identity and we'll work with you to verify ownership before deleting.
