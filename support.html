Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

Open Woofora and tap Sign In on the home screen. You can sign up with an email and password, or use Google, Apple, Facebook, or Microsoft sign-in.

How do I add my dog?

Tap the My Dogs tab and follow the 7-step wizard. You'll enter your dog's breed, size, age, weight, activity level, and chew strength. This helps Woofora recommend the right products.

How do I change my dog's profile?

Go to My Dogs, tap your dog, then tap the pencil icon. From there you can edit name, breed, size, weight, activity level, or chew strength.

How do I take the product quiz?

On the home screen, tap Find the Perfect Products. The 5-question quiz generates a personalized list of products matched to your dog.

How do I save products to a wishlist?

On any product detail screen, tap the heart icon. You can organize saved products into named wishlists (e.g. "Birthday Gifts", "Travel Kit").

How do affiliate links work?

Tapping Buy on Amazon opens Amazon with a Woofora affiliate tag attached. Amazon pays Woofora a small commission if you make a purchase — at no extra cost to you. This is how we keep the app free.

Are prices accurate?

Prices shown in Woofora are estimates based on data we've collected. The actual price is shown on Amazon's product page after you tap the link.

Why are some products marked "Light", "Moderate", "Power", or "Tough"?

These are durability ratings — our estimate of how well a toy holds up to chewing for each chew strength level. Power Chewer dogs need Tough toys.

How do I sign out?

Go to Profile → Settings → Sign Out.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Settings → Account → Delete Account. Or follow the instructions on our Account Deletion page.

How do I turn off push notifications?

Go to Profile and toggle the Push Notifications switch off.

  • What you were trying to do
  • Your iOS or Android version
  • A screenshot, if possible
